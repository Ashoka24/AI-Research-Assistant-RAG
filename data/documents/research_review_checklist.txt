Research Review Checklist

1. Identify the original source and review its date, authorship, methods and limitations.
2. Check that the cited passage directly supports the claim being made.
3. Separate a source's reported results from an analyst's interpretation.
4. Look for uncertainty, contradictory evidence and missing context.
5. Record enough source detail for another reader to reproduce the check.
6. Verify consequential claims against the full original material.
